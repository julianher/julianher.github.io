001/03

H1B Strategic Analysis

FY2025 U.S. labor market demand, mapped end-to-end.

An end-to-end BI project built on the U.S. Department of Labor's FY2025 Q3 H-1B Labor Condition Application data. 83,000+ filings cleaned in Tableau Prep, then composed into a two-tier dashboard. The "Summary" view pairs spatial application density with dynamic KPIs and box-plot salary distributions by state, so international candidates can cross-reference top sponsors against localised wage standards. Built to make career strategy and salary negotiation defensible, not guessed at.

002/03

World Cup Prediction Pool

Market-implied probabilities, optimised for a custom scoring rule.

A decision-science logbook for the 2026 World Cup pool at Coempopular (~650 players). Rather than out-guessing the betting market, it treats market-implied odds as the best available estimator and squeezes an edge from two things rivals ignore: applying those odds systematically across all 104 matches, and optimising each pick for the pool's 5/2/2/1 scoring rule instead of for the likeliest scoreline. The pipeline de-vigs the odds, solves Poisson scoring rates, builds a Dixon-Coles score matrix, and picks the scoreline that maximises expected points, all re-implemented to run live in an interactive widget you can drive yourself.

003/03

AI Customer Support Ecosystem

A multi-modal AI assistant for FIU campus parking.

Two AI interfaces over a single shared knowledge base: "Paws Support" handles typed inquiries through Expertise AI, and "Roary" handles voice through ElevenLabs with a calm, empathetic persona tuned for stressful topics like citations. The knowledge base was assembled via Gemini Deep Research, designed to handle routine inquiries cleanly while leaving complex cases like appeals to human staff.
