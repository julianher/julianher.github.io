001/03

H1B Strategic Analysis

FY2025 U.S. labor market demand, mapped end-to-end.

An end-to-end BI project built on the U.S. Department of Labor's FY2025 Q3 H-1B Labor Condition Application data. 83,000+ filings cleaned in Tableau Prep, then composed into a two-tier dashboard. The "Summary" view pairs spatial application density with dynamic KPIs and box-plot salary distributions by state, so international candidates can cross-reference top sponsors against localised wage standards. Built to make career strategy and salary negotiation defensible, not guessed at.
