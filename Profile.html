H1B Strategic Analysis

FY2025 U.S. labor market demand, mapped end-to-end.

003/03

World Cup Prediction Pool

Market-implied probabilities, optimised for a custom scoring rule.

A decision-science logbook for the 2026 World Cup pool at Coempopular (~650 players). It treats market-implied odds as the best available estimator and finds an edge in applying them systematically and optimising each pick for the pool's 5/2/2/1 rule rather than the likeliest scoreline. The pipeline (de-vig, solve Poisson rates, build a Dixon-Coles score matrix, maximise expected points) is re-implemented to run live in an interactive widget you can drive yourself.
