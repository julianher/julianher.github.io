Selected work / 003 AI Customer Support Ecosystem

An AI Customer Support Ecosystem for Campus Parking.

A multi-modal AI assistant for Florida International University's parking and transportation services, combining a text-based chatbot and a voice agent to handle routine inquiries that would otherwise occupy human staff.

Type Multi-modal AI assistant
Tools Expertise AI · ElevenLabs · Gemini
Context FIU Parking & Transportation

02Overview

Routine inquiries, handled at scale.

University parking and transportation offices field a high volume of repetitive questions like permit policies, visitor rules, citation appeals, and shuttle schedules. Most are answerable from publicly documented policies, but they consume staff time that could be spent on edge cases that actually require human judgment.

The response was to build two complementary AI interfaces over a single shared knowledge base. Paws Support, branded with FIU blue, handles typed inquiries through Expertise AI. Roary, modeled after FIU's panther mascot, handles spoken interactions through ElevenLabs with a deliberately calm and empathetic persona tuned for stressful topics like citations. The knowledge base itself was assembled by using Gemini Deep Research to gather public policy information, then structured into Q&A format, a choice that avoided pulling directly from FIU's official site while still grounding the assistants in real institutional context.

04Design choices worth noting

Three decisions that shaped the build.

001/03

Two modalities, two personas.

Voice and text interactions have different tonal expectations; trying to use one personality for both would degrade either. Roary's voice persona was tuned for emotional context that text doesn't require.

002/03

Ethical data sourcing.

Rather than scraping the official FIU site directly, I generated a parallel knowledge base from public sources via Gemini, simulating institutional documentation without overstepping.

003/03

Realistic scope.

The assistants are designed to handle the routine ~90% of inquiries cleanly and to acknowledge their limits on complex cases like appeals, payments, and anything requiring real judgment, where human agents remain essential.
